# ice-slide: design content

genre: {'family': 'puzzle', 'node': 'logic-puzzle', 'ending': 'finite', 'session_profile': 'casual'}
core_loop: Read the level -> pick a direction -> the penguin slides until it hits a wall, a door or the goal -> keys unlock their doors and crumbling tiles vanish behind you -> reach the goal inside the move budget for up to three stars -> the next level adds a rule (a key, a door, a crumbling tile) or tightens the budget.

unit_kind: level  generation: authored  units: 24
progression: linear-levels  difficulty: level-authored  axes: ['depth', 'move-limit', 'board-complexity', 'piece-variety']
win: The penguin's slide ends exactly on the goal tile.
lose: Moves-left reaches zero before the goal is reached, or the penguin slides onto a tile that already crumbled.
mastery: A better player reads the whole level before the first slide - the walls, keys, doors and crumbling tiles already mapped - and commits to a short, exact sequence instead of feeling out the board one slide at a time.
