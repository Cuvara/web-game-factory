## M13 — status

`5417d2b` feat(workflow): route-scoped loop limits and a run-level developer budget (M13,
P1-7, P1-8). Committed by its agent in its worktree; **not reviewed by the lead, not merged.**

What it implements:

- `max_visits_by_route` (engine, generic): develop in new-game gets
  `{request-changes: 2, fail: 2, iterate: 2}` and `max_visits: 7`; review, sdk, sdk-review,
  verify and prototype-review get `max_visits: 7`. Loop-limit stops are recorded as a
  structured `state.blocked_reason` (old "loop limit" message form still read).
- `factory.develop.budget: {max_sessions, max_cost, cost_from: {jsonl_key}}`, off by
  default, snapshotted into run params (`develop_budget`), sessions and costs counted from
  `events.jsonl` so a resume does not reset them; `wgf resume <run> --budget-sessions N |
  --budget-cost X` records a BUDGET_RAISED event, refused from inside a step (automation).
- Generic engine hooks: `engine.resume(operator_events=...)`, `context.entered_by`,
  `context.visit_budget`, `context.read_events()`; STEP_STARTED carries `entered_by`.
