# web-game-factory 2.6.0: production-quality games, proven from the running build

Released 2026-10-02. Merged as Factory PRs #17, #18 and #20. Template work: Cuvara/web-game-template
#19 (merged), plus the golden-port branches the goldens pin.

## Why this release exists

A real 2.5.0 `/new-game` run produced a three.js goalkeeper game that passed every check:
install, conformance, typecheck, lint, unit, build, smoke, review. Its owner could not see or
understand it:
- it rendered at about 4 % brightness;
- it lost by itself 2.8 s into play;
- a shot was never seen in flight;
- the objective was never shown;
- the keeper was a cube.

Every check was about the code. Nothing played the game, looked at a frame, or asked whether
an asset was real, loaded and on screen.

2.6.0 makes the Factory **play, measure and look at the running build** at every stage, and
refuse anything that is a developer prototype.

## What changed

**Workflow 5** (`core/workflows/new-game.workflow.yaml`):

```
init -> greybox -> greybox-playability -> assets -> develop -> playability
     -> production-quality -> visual-qa -> review -> sdk -> sdk-review -> verify -> G4 -> release
```

- **Playability** (PR #17): a bot plays every build from outside, through the game's play
  probe, with real pointer, touch and key input on desktop and mobile. It checks:
  - the objective is on screen;
  - there is no loss inside the idle grace;
  - every action is visibly acknowledged;
  - a win is reachable, or the objective rises;
  - a loss is reachable, and retry works;
  - entities are readable and frames are lit.
- **Greybox first** (PR #18): the whole loop is built with primitives and played before any
  asset is made.
- **Production assets** (PR #18):
  - the assets step works from the design's `build_spec.assets`;
  - backends: a licensed library, an agent SVG author, a 3D model author (model spec →
    pinned Blender → GLB), and placeholders, which are always flagged;
  - every file is judged: SVG, raster, font, GLB, and since #20, audio.
- **production-quality** (PR #18):
  - per asset: exists → referenced → loaded → rendered → visible on screen (checked against
    frame pixels);
  - no primitive stand-ins for readable entities, unless the design's
    `visual_identity.primitive_style` says the art direction is geometric;
  - UI measured: target sizes, overlap, contrast, styled controls, states;
  - since #20, `audio.plays`.
- **visual-qa** (PR #18): a vision judge with a rubric that fails a developer-prototype look;
  in golden runs, a baseline judge against the inspected reference frames.
- **Release** refuses unless both production reports passed for the released commit.
- **Mechanical checks** (PR #20), each replacing a defect a person caught by eye:
  - font glyph coverage per locale;
  - distinct and counted variants;
  - entity contrast on screen;
  - counted requirements derived from the rules.
- **Design depth** (PR #20, game-design 1.7.0): meta loop, goal ladder, content schedule,
  first-session target, return hooks; all archetypes state real depth.
- **Game audio** (PR #20): music and SFX are production assets, checked as files and as
  sound during play.
- **Plugin skills** (PR #20, adapter binding 1.6.0), distilled from the two reference games:
  - `production-art-2d`, `production-art-3d`, `game-ui-kit`, `juice`, `production-wiring`;
  - `retention-and-progression`, `game-audio`.
- **Autonomous profile** (PR #20): configures the three agents workflow 5 needs - the 2D
  asset author, the 3D model author and the visual-QA judge - as verified read-only headless
  Claude argvs.

**Reference games.** The golden ports are now finished games that the goldens build, play and
judge, and that fail if their art regresses to primitives:
- **Tower Merge Rush (2D):** 10 distinct riso-arcade towers, backdrop, VFX, an OFL
  Latin+Cyrillic font, music and SFX.
- **Neon Drift Arena (3D):** Blender-built craft, barriers, track and skyline, lighting,
  fonts, adaptive synthwave.

## Fixes found on the way

- **A gate bypass.** A whole-workflow run resumed under a newer definition ended COMPLETED
  and skipped review, verify and G4. Its scope now follows the definition.
- **sdk refused seam files it had nothing to erase in.** They had been deleted by init's pin
  commit.
- **The golden reviewer failed review on any develop loop.**
- **Integration fixes:**
  - library GLBs are now judged instead of skipped;
  - fonts are judged as fonts;
  - variant drawings map to their requirement;
  - the model author is asked only when configured.

## Validation

- **Release gate.** `WGF_GOLDEN=1 bin/wgf test-core --strict` passes all 9 categories, 661 tests,
  on each part's tree before its PR. The 2D and 3D goldens pass 11/11 each, with
  production-quality (incl. `audio.plays`) and visual-QA PASS.
- **CI.** The acceptance job is green on #17 and #18; #20 has its own run.
- **Full unit suite.** 2158 tests OK on the final tree.
- **Real agent runs:**
  - the SVG author: 3/3 drawings accepted;
  - the model author through Blender 4.5.14: a 10-part craft, `primitive_only` false;
  - the visual-QA judge: its verdict parsed;
  - recorded under `~/wgf-runs/autonomous-profile-2026-10-02/`.

## Upgrading from 2.5.x

**Supervised installations (the shipped default).** Nothing to configure:
- the new agents default to `kind: none`;
- the assets step falls back to flagged placeholders, which production-quality refuses;
- visual-qa blocks for a person rather than passing.

Configure `factory.assets.author`, `factory.assets.model_author` and `factory.visualqa.judge`
(commented examples in `factory.yaml`), or use the autonomous profile.

**Autonomous projects.** Re-copy `workspace/config/profiles/autonomous.yaml`. It now carries
the three new agents. The 3D model author needs Blender 4.5 on `PATH` or `WGF_BLENDER`.

**Schema changes are additive:**
- game-design 1.7.0;
- asset-manifest 1.4.0;
- playability-report 1.1.0;
- play-probe fields;
- new `production-quality-report` and `visual-qa-report`.

## Not covered

- **G4, G6 and G7 stay human.** A `/new-game` run stops at G4.
- **Golden-port branches on template `main`.** Template `main` restructured the golden ports to
  contract 2. Bringing the production golden ports onto it is in progress (one template PR
  superseding #20/#21). Meanwhile the Factory pins the golden-port commits directly.
- **Audio.** The music is Ogg Opus, which older Safari cannot play; an MP3/M4A alternative
  is a follow-up. Nobody had listened to the music before release; it was judged by
  structure and measured levels.
- **Agent spend.** There is no aggregate budget for asset author and judge calls, only
  per-call caps.
